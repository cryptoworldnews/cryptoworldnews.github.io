Ondo turns BlackRock portfolio strategies into onchain tokens

Ondo launched three onchain portfolios based on BlackRock models, giving eligible non-US investors one token for diversified exposure and visible rebalancing.

Blockchain Post EditorialSep 24, 20262 min read

Ondo Finance launched three onchain portfolio tokens on Sept. 24, using model strategies developed by BlackRock for Ondo to give eligible investors outside the United States exposure to diversified baskets through a single token. The launch shifts the job of assembling and rebalancing a basket from the investor to Ondo’s product rules and smart contracts.

What do the three tokens represent?

They cover income, diversified growth and high growth. Ondo names them High Income Powered by BlackRock (BLKHIon), Diversified Growth Powered by BlackRock (BLKDIGon) and High Growth Powered by BlackRock (BLKGRWon). The strategies are models supplied by BlackRock for Ondo’s use; they are not portfolios managed by BlackRock. Ondo’s launch description says the assets and target weights are set at inception, then rebalanced on a fixed schedule.

Ondo says the portfolios use its tokenized equities and ETFs, which are backed by securities sourced from market liquidity. The company determines how each model is implemented in its product, then encodes allocation, rebalancing and fee rules in smart contracts. In effect, the token works like a pre-packed basket: holders gain exposure to the whole allocation without buying and maintaining every position themselves.

How does one token track a changing basket?

Investors mint or redeem the portfolio token to move into or out of the exposure, rather than trading each underlying position separately. Ondo says the basket’s holdings, weights and rebalances are visible onchain, and the tokens can move peer-to-peer between wallets and across exchanges and DeFi protocols. The token represents economic exposure to the basket; it does not give investors a direct claim on the underlying funds or securities.

That setup automates portfolio changes under a preset schedule, but the model and the tokenized portfolio are not guaranteed to stay identical. BlackRock’s role is limited to providing nondiscretionary strategies based on specifications from Ondo. According to the launch disclosure, BlackRock does not choose or manage the onchain portfolios and is not responsible for their issuance, custody or operation. It also has no general obligation to update a strategy after initially providing it, so an onchain portfolio may not reflect later changes to the model.

Who can use the portfolios?

The tokens are available to eligible investors outside the United States in permitted jurisdictions, subject to local restrictions. The Block reported that Ondo Global Markets issues the portfolio tokens and Ondo Finance tokenizes them. Their transferability and DeFi use therefore come with eligibility limits; the launch does not make them universally available to crypto wallet holders. Ondo said it plans to add more onchain portfolios over time.
