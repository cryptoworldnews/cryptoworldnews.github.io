Did my ShibaSwap deposit fail, or are LP tokens hidden?

ShibaSwap LP tokens not showing in MetaMask? A successful Dig transaction means the deposit worked; import its minted LP contract on the same network to see your balance.

Merkle Street NewsroomNov 19, 2026 · 20:41 UTC#9839554 min read

Open the shiba swap Dig transaction on the right-chain explorer before treating a missing MetaMask balance as a failed deposit. A successful transaction can mint LP tokens that MetaMask keeps hidden until you import their contract address.

Each liquidity pool has its own ERC-20 pair contract, which mints LP tokens to represent a provider’s share. MetaMask shows custom tokens after you import them on the matching network. For more on how shiba swap handles liquidity positions, the linked topic covers the platform’s role in this process and where to go further: shiba swap.

Confirm the Dig transaction and import its LP token

Have your transaction hash and MetaMask set to the network where you made the deposit. You will use the explorer to check the transaction and find the pool’s LP contract address in its token transfers.

  1. Open the transaction on the right explorer. Paste the hash into Etherscan for an Ethereum deposit or Shibariumscan for a Shibarium deposit. The transaction page identifies the chain and shows its status.
  2. Read the transaction status. A status of Success means the transaction executed. If it says Failed, the deposit did not complete; the tokens you tried to deposit remain in your wallet, though you spent gas to submit the failed transaction.
  3. Find the LP-token mint in ERC-20 transfers. In the successful transaction’s ERC-20 transfer list, look for a token transfer from the zero address, shown as 0x000… or a longer zero-address form, to your wallet. That transfer is the mint: the pair contract created LP tokens and sent them to you. Its token entry identifies the LP contract.
  4. Copy the minted token’s contract address. Open the token details for that transfer and copy the contract address, making sure it belongs to the pool you deposited into. The address is the identifier MetaMask needs to show this pool’s LP token.
  5. Import the token in MetaMask on the same network. Switch MetaMask to the deposit’s network, choose Import tokens, and paste the LP contract address. MetaMask should recognize the token and display your balance. When that balance appears, the LP-token check is complete.

The explorer result tells you whether the deposit succeeded; the mint transfer tells you which LP token to import. A successful Dig transaction with a mint to your wallet means the position was created even when MetaMask’s token list did not include it.

Related questions about ShibaSwap tokens

Why doesn't MetaMask show my xSHIB?

MetaMask may not list xSHIB until you import its token contract on the network where you hold it. First confirm that the transaction created or transferred xSHIB to your wallet, then use the relevant token contract address to import it. The LP-token mint described above applies to a pool’s pair token; xSHIB is a different token, so use its own contract address.

Why is my LP token balance such a small number?

The displayed number is the amount of LP tokens minted for your share of that pool, not the number or value of the two underlying tokens you deposited. Each pool issues its own LP token, and the number of tokens is not a direct readout of your deposit’s market value. Confirm the token contract and balance before drawing conclusions from the size of the number.

What to check beyond a wallet display

This check covers one Dig deposit and whether its LP tokens were minted to your wallet. If the transaction succeeded but the transfer list shows no mint to your address, importing a token will not establish that you received a wallet balance. Also, LP tokens deposited into a farm are held by the farm contract while staked, so they will not appear as an unstaked wallet balance. A successful transaction, the right mint recipient, and the matching network determine what MetaMask can show.

Questions to ask next

How do I see what my ShibaSwap LP tokens are worth?

Start by identifying the pool and the amount of each underlying token represented by your LP balance. A token count alone does not give the position’s value; that depends on the pool’s assets and their current values. Use information for the specific pool and network when assessing it.

Can I send ShibaSwap LP tokens to another wallet?

LP tokens are ERC-20 tokens, so a wallet can send them to another address on the same network. The recipient needs the correct network and may need to import the LP contract to see the balance. Sending them transfers the pool share represented by those tokens, so verify the address and token before sending.
