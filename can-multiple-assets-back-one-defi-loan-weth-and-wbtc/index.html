Can multiple assets back one DeFi loan? WETH and WBTC

Can multiple assets back one DeFi lending position? Yes. Eligible assets share collateral value, while each asset’s price and risk settings shape borrowing room.

Merkle Street NewsroomNov 28, 2026 · 11:55 UTC#fb47d03 min read

Yes. A SparkLend position can use multiple eligible supplied assets as collateral for one borrow, with each asset’s value and risk settings helping determine how much the position can borrow and its health factor. The assets work together in the same position when they are enabled as collateral.

How do WETH and WBTC support one DAI borrow?

A shared position combines eligible collateral balances when the borrower enables each asset as collateral. SparkLend uses Aave V3 lending-market mechanics: the supplied assets sit in the position, and the borrower can borrow a listed asset against them.

  1. Supply WETH and WBTC to the same position.
  2. Enable each asset as collateral in the position settings, subject to its eligibility and any applicable special mode.
  3. Borrow DAI, if it is listed and the position has enough borrowing capacity.

Both WETH and WBTC then back the DAI borrow. Think of them as two items in one basket: the position considers the basket’s collateral value, but each item has its own risk settings. Token units are not added together as if one WETH and one WBTC were interchangeable.

This is a constructed use case: a borrower needs DAI for a planned expense and wants to keep WETH and WBTC supplied rather than sell them. Combining eligible assets can let both support the same borrow. The publication’s article on managing a SparkLend position covers three actions borrowers can take as their position changes.

How does a collateral mix change borrowing room?

A collateral mix affects borrowing room through each asset’s price and risk parameters, including its LTV and liquidation threshold. The position’s capacity and health factor reflect the mix, rather than treating every dollar of collateral as having identical risk settings.

Suppose the borrower supplies WETH and WBTC, then borrows DAI. If WBTC’s price falls, its collateral value falls too. That can reduce the position’s borrowing headroom and weaken its health factor even if WETH’s price holds steady. A price drop in either asset can affect the shared position.

The reverse also matters: a larger balance of one asset does not automatically cancel the risk attached to another. Each asset contributes according to its own value and parameters. Before borrowing, a user should consider how a price move in either asset would affect the combined position, and leave room for that change.

This is why the collateral mix matters even when the borrower thinks of the loan as one amount of DAI. The position links that borrow to all enabled collateral, so a weaker asset can reduce borrowing headroom while the other asset remains unchanged. The health factor reflects the combined position’s risk.

Related questions

How does a collateral mix affect a DeFi lending health factor?

A DeFi lending health factor reflects the position’s collateral and debt together, including the value and risk settings of each enabled asset. A price move in either WETH or WBTC can change the mix and make the position less healthy.

How can I tell whether an asset is eligible as collateral?

An asset is eligible when the protocol lists it for use as collateral and the position settings allow it to be enabled. Confirm eligibility and any special mode that applies before relying on that asset to support a borrow.
