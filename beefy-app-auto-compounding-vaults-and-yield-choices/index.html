Beefy app: Auto-Compounding Vaults and Yield Choices

How to decide if the Beefy app is right for you: use it for hands-off compounding when its fees, chain and layered contract risk fit your plan.

Merkle Street NewsroomNov 28, 2026 · 03:03 UTC#2e7eb85 min read

The Beefy app fits holders who want DeFi rewards compounded automatically and accept the added smart contract risk; its vaults harvest rewards and reinvest them without repeated manual claim-and-deposit cycles. For more on this choice, the Beefy app helps you decide whether this approach to yield fits your goals.

Beefy Vaults suit holders who want rewards reinvested

Beefy Vaults automate a cycle: a supported DeFi strategy earns rewards, the vault harvests them, then the rewards are compounded into the strategy. The result is less routine work for the holder, who does not need to claim and reinvest each round manually.

That automation can matter especially for small deposits. When a holder farms manually, each claim-and-reinvest cycle can require a transaction and its gas cost. With a Beefy vault, harvest gas is shared across depositors in the vault rather than paid separately by each one. So a small depositor can benefit from compounding without personally covering the full gas cost of every harvest. The performance fee Beefy takes from harvested rewards still applies, and chain gas and any ZAP swap fees can also affect the result.

Picture a holder with a modest deposit who has little time to monitor a farm. In a vault, the harvest-and-compound work happens as part of the shared strategy, so they avoid making each reinvestment transaction themselves. The trade-off is that their funds rely on both the underlying farm and Beefy’s automation contracts. The added protocol layer brings its own smart contract risk, on top of the farm’s risk.

Manual farming and other optimizers trade automation for control

Manual farming gives the holder direct control over when to claim rewards and what to do with them. That fits someone who wants to keep reward tokens, choose when to sell or reinvest, or avoid adding an optimizer’s contracts to the risk chain. It also takes time and can mean paying transaction gas for each claim and reinvestment.

Other optimizers, such as Yearn Finance or Harvest Finance, offer another route to automated yield strategies. Compare a specific vault with a Beefy Vault by checking which chains it supports, how its fees work, and how much control you retain over reward handling. Beefy operates across multiple chains, including Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Arbitrum and Base; support varies by product and strategy, so check the chain for the vault you are considering.

Costs depend on the chosen vault and chain. Beefy’s performance fee comes from harvested rewards, while transaction gas depends on the chain and activity. A ZAP may add swap fees when it converts assets as part of a deposit or withdrawal. For another optimizer or manual farming, compare the applicable fees and transaction costs for the specific strategy; the product name alone does not tell you which option will cost less.

Choose by deposit size, time, risk tolerance and chain

A Beefy yield farming app is a reasonable fit when you want hands-off compounding, the vault supports your chain and you accept the combined risk of the farm and automation contracts. Manual farming fits better when keeping reward tokens or choosing each reinvestment matters more than saving time. Skip both approaches when the available strategy’s risk or costs do not fit your plan.

Use four checks to decide. First, consider deposit size: shared harvest gas can make automation useful even for a small deposit, though fees still affect net results. Second, consider time: less time for repeated claim-and-reinvest cycles favors a vault. Third, consider risk tolerance: automation adds a protocol layer, so a holder comfortable with the underlying farm alone may not want the added exposure. Fourth, confirm the chain and vault support the assets and strategy you intend to use.

The rule is straightforward: choose a Beefy vault for hands-off compounding on a supported chain; farm manually if you want to keep reward tokens and direct control. Before depositing, compare the specific vault’s performance fee, the chain’s gas costs and any ZAP swap fees, then weigh them against the time and transaction work you save. That check tells you whether to use a vault, farm manually or sit out.

Questions readers ask next

How are Beefy APYs calculated?

Beefy vault APYs estimate the return from a strategy’s yield and compounding over time. They can change as farm rewards, asset prices, vault activity and fees change, so treat an APY as an estimate rather than a guaranteed return.

What is the BIFI token used for?

BIFI is Beefy’s platform token, used in Beefy’s token and governance-related features. Its role is separate from the underlying assets deposited in a Beefy Vault, so check the current details before treating BIFI as part of a vault’s yield.

Which chain is cheapest to use Beefy on?

There is no single cheapest chain for every use: gas costs vary by chain and over time, while the selected strategy and any ZAP swaps can add costs. Compare the expected transaction and vault fees for the specific chain and strategy you plan to use.
