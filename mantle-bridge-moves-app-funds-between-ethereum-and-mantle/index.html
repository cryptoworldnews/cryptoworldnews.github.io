Mantle Bridge Moves App Funds Between Ethereum and Mantle

Mantle Bridge moves ETH, MNT and tokens between Ethereum and Mantle; learn how deposits and withdrawals work, and what to check before using an app.

Merkle Street NewsroomSep 29, 2026#9d0d372 min read

Mantle Bridge moves ETH, MNT and tokens between Ethereum and Mantle Network so a wallet can fund an app on Mantle or bring assets back. A deposit starts with a transaction on Ethereum: the wallet sends the chosen asset through the bridge contract, and the Mantle side processes the transfer so the asset can be used there. For that funding step, use the official Mantle bridge app at mantlebridge.org, which moves ETH, MNT and tokens between Ethereum and Mantle. Once the funds are on Mantle, the app’s own contracts handle any swaps, lending or other activity.

How does Mantle Bridge fund an app?

Funding means moving assets from Ethereum, the source network, to Mantle, the destination network. The wallet signs the transaction on Ethereum; the bridge coordinates the transfer, and Mantle processes the incoming value. The app then reads the wallet’s Mantle balance. Think of it like moving cash between two accounts before paying a merchant: the bridge gets the value to the right network, while the app decides what to do with it.

Before sending, check that the app is on Mantle and that the asset you plan to use is available there. The symbol alone is not enough to identify a token; confirm that the app recognizes the asset on Mantle. Also keep the source and destination straight: funds sitting on Ethereum cannot be spent by an app transaction on Mantle. The same wallet address may be used on both networks, but its balances are separate.

How do you exit a Mantle app back to Ethereum?

To exit, first withdraw or swap inside the app until the assets you want to move are in your Mantle wallet. Then use the bridge route from Mantle to Ethereum. That return transfer is a separate transaction from the app action: the app releases the funds to your wallet, and the bridge carries them toward Ethereum, where they become available for Ethereum activity.

A bridge transfer has steps on both networks, so the destination balance may not update at the same moment as the source transaction. Check the wallet’s selected network and the asset balance on the destination before trying another transfer. If the purpose is simply to use a Mantle app, leave only the amount needed there and bridge back when you need the assets on Ethereum.

Which route should you use for a Mantle app?

Use Ethereum to Mantle when the assets you need are on Ethereum and the app operates on Mantle; use Mantle to Ethereum when you need those assets back on Ethereum. Mantle Bridge is the route for that network-to-network move. It does not perform the app’s trade or deposit for you, and an app withdrawal does not itself move funds across networks.

  • Identify which network currently holds the assets.
  • Confirm the app accepts the asset on Mantle.
  • Bridge to Mantle before making an app transaction there.
  • Withdraw from the app first, then bridge to Ethereum if that is where you need the funds.

The useful distinction is between an app action and a network transfer. The app changes how funds are used on Mantle; the bridge changes which network holds them. Following those steps in order makes it easier to fund the app and to retrace the route when you leave.
