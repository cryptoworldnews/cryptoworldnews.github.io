How Mantle Bridge Moves Assets Between Ethereum and Mantle

Mantle Bridge moves assets between Ethereum and Mantle through wallet transactions, token approvals and a separate withdrawal settlement and claim step.

Merkle Street NewsroomOct 21, 2026 · 14:42 UTC#b2e12b4 min read

Mantle Bridge moves assets between Ethereum and Mantle by having your wallet send a transaction on the source network, then processing a message so the matching asset appears on the destination. To deposit, you choose Ethereum as the source and Mantle as the destination; to withdraw, you reverse them. The bridge does not swap one asset for another. It transfers a supported asset between networks, where it may have a corresponding token representation.

Start by connecting the wallet that holds the funds, selecting the direction, token and amount, then checking the receiving address. For a quick reminder of the route, Mantle Bridge is the name to keep in view when checking the selected networks and destination. The wallet must also be connected to the correct source network before it can submit the transaction.

How do you deposit to Mantle Bridge?

A deposit starts with a transaction on Ethereum, which pays Ethereum gas and instructs the bridge to transfer the selected asset. For an ERC-20 token such as a stablecoin, the first transaction may be an approval: it gives the bridge contract permission to spend up to a stated amount of that token. Approval alone does not transfer funds. After it confirms, submit the deposit transaction and review its amount, destination address and network in the wallet prompt.

Once Ethereum records the deposit, a cross-network message carries the instruction to Mantle. When that message is processed, the corresponding balance becomes available at the destination address. The interface shows the transfer’s status; a source transaction marked successful does not always mean the destination balance has appeared yet.

Before sending, check these details:

  • The source is Ethereum and the destination is Mantle.
  • The token and amount are correct on both the bridge screen and wallet prompt.
  • The receiving address is yours and matches the address you intend to use on Mantle.
  • You have ETH for Ethereum transaction fees, including any required token approval.

After arrival, you need MNT to pay for transactions on Mantle. Bridged ETH or stablecoins do not pay Mantle gas fees. Plan how to obtain a small MNT balance before you need to move or use the deposited assets.

How do you withdraw from Mantle to Ethereum?

A withdrawal begins with a Mantle transaction, then requires the bridge’s withdrawal message to settle before the funds can be claimed on Ethereum. Choose Mantle as the source, Ethereum as the destination, and the token and amount to withdraw. Keep MNT in the wallet for the Mantle transaction fee.

After you confirm the withdrawal, the source transaction records the request. The funds are not immediately available in the Ethereum wallet. The bridge must process the message through Mantle’s settlement system; once the interface marks the withdrawal ready, you may need to submit a separate claim transaction on Ethereum. That claim uses ETH for gas. Follow the status shown for your transfer, since the steps and wait depend on the bridge’s settlement state.

This makes the two directions different in practice: a deposit is usually a send followed by destination processing, while a withdrawal can include a later claim. Keep the transaction details and check the destination wallet before repeating a transfer that appears delayed.

What should you check before using Mantle Bridge?

Check the network, receiving address, token and amount at each step, because a confirmed transaction on one network cannot simply be reversed by changing the bridge screen. Use only assets the interface offers for the selected direction, and read each wallet prompt before signing. For ERC-20 approvals, confirm the token and allowance amount; for transfers, confirm the actual transaction details.

The practical choice is straightforward: use the direct bridge route when you want the corresponding asset on Mantle or Ethereum and can wait for its processing steps. Budget for gas on the network where each transaction happens, and treat deposit, withdrawal initiation and withdrawal claim as distinct actions. Knowing which step is pending makes it easier to tell whether funds are still moving or ready to use.
