BIFI Maxi vs BIFI Pool: How Their Rewards Work

BIFI Maxi vs BIFI Pool: choose Maxi to compound rewards into BIFI automatically, or the Pool to claim protocol revenue in another token and pay gas.

Merkle Street NewsroomNov 28, 2026 · 04:11 UTC#1709c33 min read

Choose BIFI Maxi to grow your BIFI balance automatically; choose the BIFI Pool to receive protocol revenue in another token. The difference comes down to what you want to receive and whether you want rewards compounded for you or claimed manually.

In BIFI Maxi, rewards go back into BIFI, so your BIFI position can grow over time. Your share is represented through mooTokens. In the BIFI Pool, your BIFI stays fixed while the Pool distributes a share of protocol fee revenue in a separate, non-BIFI token. You claim those rewards yourself.

Do you want more BIFI or another reward token?

BIFI Maxi fits a goal of accumulating more BIFI, while the BIFI Pool fits a goal of receiving income in another asset. The reward asset changes how your position is exposed to BIFI’s price.

  • BIFI Maxi: Rewards compound back into BIFI, increasing the BIFI exposure of your position. Your mooTokens represent the position as it grows.
  • BIFI Pool: Your BIFI balance stays fixed, and protocol fee revenue is paid in a non-BIFI token that you must claim.

That separate reward token gives you income outside BIFI, which diversifies the form of what you receive. It does not remove the price exposure of the BIFI you keep in the Pool. Maxi concentrates both your original BIFI and compounded rewards in BIFI. Say BIFI’s price falls: that fall affects the value of your principal and the rewards accumulated in Maxi together.

The Beefy app is the interface for Beefy’s multichain yield optimizer. Its Beefy app coverage of BIFI Maxi and the BIFI Pool can take this comparison further by explaining how each option handles rewards.

Would you rather compound automatically or claim rewards?

BIFI Maxi compounds without manual reward claims; the BIFI Pool requires you to claim, and each claim costs gas. That cost matters more when the amount you are claiming is small.

With Maxi, rewards feed into the BIFI position automatically, so you do not need to make a separate claim to compound them. With the Pool, rewards accrue in another token and you decide when to claim them. Claiming more often means paying gas more often; waiting means leaving rewards unclaimed for longer. The value of a claim relative to its gas cost depends on the size of your Pool rewards and the cost of the transaction when you claim.

Think of the two options as different ways of handling the same decision: Maxi rolls rewards back into the position, while the Pool pays them out separately for you to collect. The Beefy app gives BIFI holders access to a multichain yield optimizer that automatically compounds rewards from supported DeFi strategies; the choice between these options still depends on the reward form and claim trade-off that suits you.

Which option matches your goal?

Choose BIFI Maxi when you want more BIFI and prefer automatic compounding. Choose the BIFI Pool when you want protocol revenue in another token and are willing to claim it and pay gas.

  • Choose BIFI Maxi for rewards compounded into BIFI, no manual claims, and a position concentrated in BIFI.
  • Choose the BIFI Pool for a fixed BIFI balance and separate-token rewards, with manual claims and gas costs to consider.

If your priority is increasing your BIFI count hands-off, Maxi matches that goal. If your priority is receiving income outside BIFI, the Pool matches that goal. Compare the reward asset, compounding, claim costs and BIFI concentration before choosing where to put your BIFI.
