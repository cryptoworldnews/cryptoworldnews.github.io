About Blockchain Post

Blockchain Post covers crypto as a set of working machines: blockchains, exchanges, stablecoins, lending protocols, wallets and the rules that govern them. Each story explains how the relevant system works before reporting what changed, so every headline arrives with its mechanism attached.

We are independent and not owned or paid by any project we cover. We work from primary sources such as filings, code, on-chain records and official statements, and we say where each claim comes from. When we get something wrong, we correct the article and note the change. This publication is independent and nothing here is financial advice.
