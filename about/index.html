About Merkle Street

Merkle Street is an independent newsroom reporting on crypto across the whole field: Bitcoin and Ethereum, layer 2 networks, DeFi, stablecoins, exchanges, regulation and security incidents. Each story walks through how the thing actually works before getting to what it means.

We report from primary sources where we can, including filings, code repositories, on-chain records and official statements, and we say plainly when something is unconfirmed. When we get something wrong, we correct the article and note the change. We are not owned or paid by any project we cover. Merkle Street is independent, and nothing published here is financial advice.
