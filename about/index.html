About Crypto Daily

Crypto Daily is an independent newsroom covering cryptocurrency markets, protocols, businesses and public policy. We report developments as they happen, explain the underlying mechanics and distinguish confirmed facts from claims and forecasts.

Our editorial decisions are independent of the projects and companies we cover. We prioritize original documents, on-record sources and verifiable data, attribute outside reporting and disclose relevant conflicts. We seek responses to substantive criticism and correct material errors promptly, with a clear note explaining what changed.
