About Crypto Operations Desk

Crypto Operations Desk is an independent newsroom covering protocols, staking, network reliability and the companies connecting crypto to financial markets. We test the prevailing narrative against the experience of running infrastructure.

Our reporting prioritizes primary documents, reproducible network data and attributed sources. We distinguish verified developments from claims and analysis, disclose relevant conflicts, and keep commercial interests outside editorial decisions. Material corrections are identified on the affected article with the date and an explanation of what changed.
